Realm of Catan: Turn 1 Guide

1. City & Settlement Foundation

Your capital governs adjacent hex tiles. Each turn, your controlled hexes produce resources like Wood, Ore, Gold, and Mana automatically.

2. Economy & Upgrades

Spend accumulated Wood and Ore in the Realm tab to upgrade your Lumber Mills and Iron Mines, multiplying your passive income each turn cycle.

3. Multiplayer Realm Chat

Use the built-in Chat tab to communicate with your friends in real-time, negotiate resource exchanges, and coordinate joint raids.

4. Persistent Turns & Progression

When you are finished reviewing your empire, click "Submit Turn" in the top header. Your moves lock in for the persistent world tick so you can play sporadically!

You can re-open this guide anytime using the top Guide button.
