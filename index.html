Lagoon Chronicles: Campaign Guide

1. Action Points (AP) Energy System

Each turn gives you a limited pool of Action Points (AP) to spend on exploring fogged hexes, dispatching champions, or raiding nodes. AP completely resets when you end your turn! Build Town Halls to permanently increase your max AP.

2. Fog of War & Hex Exploration

The archipelago starts covered in Fog of War. Click adjacent fogged hexes and spend 1 AP to explore them, revealing terrain artwork, resource yields, and secret encounters.

3. Random World Events & Strict Turn Locks

Random diplomatic or weather disaster events may trigger when starting a new turn. Additionally, strict turn locks ensure actions cannot be performed while waiting for the next turn cycle.

Re-open this guide anytime using the top Guide button.

End Turn & Advance World
