Lagoon Chronicles: Turn 1 Guide

1. City & Settlement Foundation

Your capital governs adjacent hex tiles. Each turn cycle, your controlled territory generates resources like Wood, Ore, Gold, and Mana automatically.

2. Economy & Upgrades

Spend Wood and Ore in the Realm tab to upgrade your Lumber Mills and Iron Mines to increase passive resource yields each turn.

3. Discord Webhook Integration

Click the Discord button in the top header to configure your server webhook. When you finish your turn, your friends in Discord will instantly receive an automated notification!

4. Persistent World Turns

Play sporadically! Review incoming trades, chat with your friends, and click "Submit Turn" when you are ready to advance the world.
